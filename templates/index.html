How Echo works

Three steps. No logins required for the demo. No wasted knowledge.

🎙

1. Leave an Echo

Record a voice note or type what you wish you knew as a junior. Tag it by course, professor, or topic.

◈

2. Echo processes it

Whisper transcribes the audio. Gemini embeds the meaning semantically. Memory Health is computed. Stored forever.

🔍

3. Ask the Batch

Ask anything in plain language. Echo finds the relevant memories and synthesises what seniors collectively said.
